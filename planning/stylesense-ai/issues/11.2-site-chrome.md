# 11.2 — Site Chrome: Two-Row Header and Footer

**What to build:** Replace the single-row header with the design's two-row composition that retracts on scroll, and give the app its first footer.

**Blocked by:** 11.1 — Typography, Design Tokens, and Document Head

**Status:** ready-for-agent

## Decisions

**Two rows, and the wordmark row retracts on scroll.** Row 1 is search, a centred wordmark, then wishlist / bag / account icons. Row 2 is the centred nav. The centred wordmark over a ruled nav row is the editorial-luxury signature; two permanently sticky rows is ~112px of chrome on every scroll of a site built for looking at photographs. Retracting the wordmark row on scroll gives the composition on arrival and the viewport back afterwards — branding only needs to land once.

**Nav is `HOME · SHOP · AI STYLIST`.** `COLLECTIONS` and `ABOUT` are out of scope and do not appear rather than shipping dead. The account icon points at `/profile`, which becomes a real destination in 11.9 — the same reasoning epics 09 and 10 used when they declined to build an account dropdown with two real entries and one stub.

**The header search icon is the first UI for a filter that already exists.** `ProductFilters` supports `search`, `FILTER_KEYS` lists it, and the API implements it as a name-plus-description `icontains`. No surface has ever exposed it.

**The footer ships with real destinations only.** Three columns plus a copyright rule: brand blurb, a Shopping column of filtered `/shop` links, and an Account / AI Stylist column. Cut from the design: the **Support** column (no shipping page, no style guide, no contact form, no route for any of them) and the **Newsletter** field (no endpoint, no model, no email service — a field that swallows an address and says nothing is worse than no field). Copyright year is computed, not the design's hardcoded `© 2024`.

**Footer renders on every `AppLayout` route except `/chat` and `/checkout`.** Chat is a full-height conversation with its own sidebar and a pinned input; checkout is a payment funnel where every outbound link is a leak. Login and Register already sit outside `AppLayout`. The design only draws a footer on Home and Shop, which would leave Cart, Orders, Wishlist and Product Detail ending in raw whitespace.

**Attribution lives here.** A single "Photography from Unsplash & Pexels" line beside the copyright, per 11.3.
