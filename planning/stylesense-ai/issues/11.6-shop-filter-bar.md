# 11.6 — Shop Filter Bar

**What to build:** Replace the left filter rail with a top bar of facet popovers over a full-width grid.

**Blocked by:** 11.2 — Site Chrome; 11.5 — Faceted Search API

**Status:** ready-for-agent

Prototype: branch **`prototype/beautiful-stylesense`** (commit `3680b78`), not on `main`. `git checkout prototype/beautiful-stylesense` then `pnpm --dir frontend dev` and open `/prototype/filter-bar?v=a|b|c|d`. Nothing to delete when this ships — the branch stays unmerged.

## Decisions

**A row of facet popovers, instant apply.** Four layouts were built and measured at 1440. The popover bar was the only one that shows counts, holds all six facets, and stays at 56px. Flat pill rows cost 230px permanently, pushing the grid below the fold, and **cannot express price at all** — a range needs a slider and a slider does not fit a pill row, so it silently ships five facets. An expanding panel needs a full draft copy of the filter state plus reconciliation on Apply. A filter sheet gives the grid the most room and hides every count behind a click, which undoes the reason 11.5 computes them.

**The chip row stays alongside the count badges.** They look redundant and are not: the badge says *how many* (`COLOUR ②`), the chips say *which* (`Black ✕` `Navy ✕`). The deciding case is price — a range has nothing to badge, so **price only ever appears as a chip**, and cutting the row makes an active price filter invisible unless the shopper reopens the popover.

**Zero-count options stay visible, greyed and disabled, showing `0`.** Hiding them changes the popover's length every time another facet is touched, so the list reflows under the cursor mid-interaction. A `0` also carries information: "Levi's 0" says Levi's makes no dresses. Disabled rather than clickable, because an option leading to an empty grid is a small betrayal.

**On `/shop` the whole header retracts and the bar takes the sticky slot.** 11.2 leaves the nav row permanently sticky; stacking a sticky filter bar under it puts ~100px of chrome on every scroll of the page built for looking at photographs. Letting the header yield keeps the sticky cost at 56px — no worse than today — and the control that survives is the useful one. Nobody deep in a filtered catalog needs a link to Home.

**Grid ladder 2 / 3 / 4, no density toggle.** Measured at 1440: 3-up gives ~400px cards, 4-up ~290px, 5-up ~225px. 3-up is more editorial and turns 50 products into 17 rows while losing across-row comparison; 4-up at ~290px is still a real photograph. The density toggle drawn in `designs/page-shop` is persisted preference state spent on a control almost nobody touches.

**Mobile is the filter sheet, portalled.** Six popovers do not fit 375px. This is an upgrade of the 300px drawer sub-ticket 4.4 already built, gaining multi-select, counts and price. **It must be portalled to `document.body`** — see the epic's `backdrop-filter` decision, which this prototype found by rendering the sheet clipped inside the sticky bar.
