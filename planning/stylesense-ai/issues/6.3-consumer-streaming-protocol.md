# 6.3 — Wire Streaming into WebSocket Consumer

**What to build:** The `ChatConsumer` receives a `chat.message` from the client, calls `ChatService.send_message_stream()`, and sends the full streaming protocol back over the WebSocket: `chat.stream_start` → `chat.token` × N → `chat.stream_end` (with `conversation_id` and `message_id`). Errors are sent as `chat.error`. The consumer enforces per-user rate limiting (5 messages/min) and rejects concurrent messages while a stream is in progress. Verifiable end-to-end with a WebSocket client tool (websocat or Postman).

**Blocked by:** 6.1 — Django Channels + WebSocket Plumbing, 6.2 — Streaming Provider + ChatService
