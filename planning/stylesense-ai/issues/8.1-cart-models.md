# 8.1 — Cart Models

**What to build:** A new `apps.cart` holding the persistent bag. `Cart` is one-per-user (authenticated-only, so a plain `OneToOneField` — no session cart, no merge-on-login). `CartItem` is one line per product *variant*: a DB-level unique constraint on `(cart, product, size, color)` means the same shirt in M and L are two lines, while the same shirt in M twice is one line with quantity 2. `size`/`color` are blank-not-null on purpose — Postgres treats `NULL != NULL`, so nullable columns would let the constraint pass for two "no size" lines of the same product.
